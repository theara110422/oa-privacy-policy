Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 3, 2026

Operation Application ( "we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information:

  • Personal Information: Full name, email address, phone number, date of birth, gender, address, emergency contact details, and national ID number.
  • Employment Information: Employee code, position, department, staff type, branch assignment, join date, and work schedule.
  • Profile Data: Profile photo and digital signature uploaded by the user.
  • Location Data: GPS coordinates collected during check-in and check-out to verify attendance at authorized school locations.
  • Attendance Data: Check-in and check-out times, attendance status, and reasons for late or early departures.
  • Device Information: Device type, operating system version, and app version for compatibility and support purposes.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information to:

  • Manage staff attendance, scheduling, and payroll operations.
  • Verify your physical location during check-in/check-out to ensure attendance integrity.
  • Process leave requests, employment contracts, and HR documents.
  • Communicate important updates, announcements, and notifications.
  • Generate salary slips, tax documents, and employment records.
  • Improve the app's functionality and user experience.
  • Ensure the security of your account and prevent unauthorized access.

3. Location Data

Important: This app collects precise location data (GPS) to verify that check-ins and check-outs occur at authorized school locations. Location data is only collected during the check-in/check-out process and is not tracked continuously in the background.

Location data is used solely for attendance verification purposes and is shared only with your school's HR administrators.

4. Data Storage and Security

  • Your data is stored on secure servers operated by your school organization.
  • We implement industry-standard encryption, access controls, and authentication mechanisms to protect your information.
  • Your session is secured using JWT (JSON Web Tokens) with automatic refresh and expiration.
  • Your PIN is never stored in plain text — it is securely hashed on the server.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell or rent your personal information to third parties. Your information may be shared only with:

  • School Administrators & HR: Attendance records, profile data, and employment information are accessible to authorized school administrators and HR staff for management purposes.
  • Legal Requirements: We may disclose your information if required by law, regulation, or legal process.

6. Data Retention

We retain your personal information for as long as your employment account is active or as needed to provide services. Attendance records, payroll data, and employment documents are retained according to your school's record-keeping policies and applicable legal requirements.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access and view your personal information stored in the app.
  • Update or correct your profile information through the app or by contacting HR.
  • Request deletion of your account and associated data (subject to legal and organizational retention requirements).
  • Opt out of non-essential data collection.

8. Children's Privacy

This app is intended for use by school staff members only. It is not directed at children under the age of 16. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any significant changes by posting the new policy within the app and updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.

10. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or our data practices, please contact your school's HR department or administrator.
